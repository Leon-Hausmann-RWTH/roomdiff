# Design note

## Principle: change needs evidence

Both videos are reconstructed together, so every keyframe has depth and a pose in one metric frame. A surface the before video saw counts as gone only if frames of the after video looked *through* the place where it was: the after depth along those rays lies beyond the point (evidence of absence, as in Fehr et al., ICRA 2017, and Khronos, RSS 2024; the per-pixel test is SceneDiff's `foreground_distance`). A nearer after depth means something blocks the view, and a place too few after frames reach was not checked; neither can produce a change. The same test runs from after to before for additions.

Borrowed: the joint feed-forward reconstruction, SAM masks, DINO matching and the depth test from SceneDiff, whose released code scores unobserved pixels as unchanged and writes 2D masks per frame. OASIS-Map keeps an unknown state per object but needs LiDAR-SLAM poses and gives no reasons or language. New here: votes per point over all frames and distinct camera positions, a verdict per object with its reason, a search for the missing end of a move whose best match counts only where the first video saw the place empty, sentences whose numbers come from 3D points, and outputs that show what was not checked.

## Decisions

1. **One joint feed-forward reconstruction.** Splat-based detectors assume a dense before capture; on SceneDiff's casual pairs 3DGS-CD reaches 0.5 obj/sc AP against 22.8 for SceneDiff's pipeline. One Pi3X pass needs no alignment: on the report pairs 1.2 % of the static background comes out free (median 0.2 %, worst 19 % on a pair whose videos share one corner). Pi3X was chosen for its metric scale, which the sentences and thresholds use; Pi3 was not compared, and Pi3X's weights are non-commercial.
2. **Ray evidence, not nearest-neighbour differencing.** Differencing (as in VGGT-CD) calls every region one video did not cover a change: on the report pairs it reports 435 changes at precision 0.08, 183 where the other video saw too little to check. The ray test reports 54 at precision 0.57 with 1 there, and none on six half-video controls of an unchanged room, where differencing reports 37. The price is recall: 0.62 against 0.72 (0.77 against 0.94 for objects 8 cm or taller).
3. **Votes from distinct camera positions.** Neighbouring keyframes see nearly the same ray, so one burst of bad depth looks like many votes. Requiring that cameras in two places looked through 30 % of a candidate removed 33 of 45 false candidates on the validation pairs and kept 14 of 17 detectable objects. A thickness rule and a free-margin rule were tried and dropped: the first rejects objects seen from one side, the second did not separate true from false candidates.
4. **Verdicts with reasons.** Each object is confirmed, unverified or dismissed from the states of its points above the contact zone, and the reason says why: partly still there, too flat to tell from its support, or hidden or seen too rarely (then a removal gets the pose to film from again; this never happened on the test pairs, since candidates start from free points). A confirmed removal or addition without a partner triggers a search of the other video; its best appearance match counts only if the first video saw that place empty. On the report pairs it found 6 moves, 5 real, and raised the share of found changes with the right type from 0.45 to 0.61. Also using places the first video never filmed gave 0.71, but 2 of those 3 moves had an end on a different object (a TV console, an identical chair).
5. **Language models only name.** VLMs are poor change detectors (7.1 against 43.8 obj/im AP in SceneDiff's comparison), so none decides anything. SigLIP 2 names masked crops in-process, with no server; supports are found in 3D and only named from an image.

## Results and failure modes

On 18 indoor SceneDiff test pairs roomdiff finds 62 % of the 50 changed objects (a quarter of a change's points within 5 cm of the lifted ground-truth object) and 77 % of those 8 cm or taller, at precision 0.57. Of the 27 ground-truth moves, 8 are found as moves, 7 of them with both ends on the object (median distance error 0.06 m); it reports 16 moves in all. The official evaluator, which needs a 2D mask IoU of 0.5, counts 16 of 50 and gives 42.7 px/im IoU, 28.0 obj/im AP and 15.7 obj/sc AP (SceneDiff: 39.1, 43.8 and 22.8 on its full set). A pair takes about 3 minutes on an M2 Max. Failure modes: flat objects stay inside the depth band of 4 cm plus 3 % of the depth (27 % recall under 8 cm); a camera that moved less than about 1 m loses changes to the two-viewpoint rule; slightly moved furniture and deformable objects split; walls misalign when the videos share little; labels and support names can be wrong. Next: an appearance cue for flat objects, and filming again where the coverage tint shows gaps.
